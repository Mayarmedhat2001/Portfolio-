About

Two-plus years shipping production backends

I design and build .NET microservices on Microsoft Azure. My work has covered two very different worlds: an enterprise platform that runs multi-agent AI workflows for finance, legal, manufacturing and government clients, and a national insurance claims platform that reports to the Central Bank of the UAE.

Both taught me the same thing. The interesting problems sit at the boundaries: who is allowed to do what, how a payload moves between services, and how you prove afterwards what happened. My side projects keep pulling on that thread, especially for AI agents that take real actions.

I work with distributed, international teams and I'm open to remote or contractor arrangements, including through an EOR.
